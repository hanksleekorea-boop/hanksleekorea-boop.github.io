Review what the public Free release includes and excludes

The current public release is $0 and requires no sign-in or credit card. Link, settings, and export data stay on this device and are not sent to a LinkMax server.

$0/monthNo credit cardNo live payment or payout

Available now

  • Safe HTTP(S) basic tracking links
  • Plans for 4 roles and 5 service types
  • Korean and English affiliate/ad disclosure
  • Sample performance with evidence and limits
  • Device save, restore, JSON export, and scoped deletion
  • PC, mobile web, and keyboard accessibility

Not included in the current public Free release

  • Live affiliate enrollment, approval, or revenue links
  • Server accounts or automatic cross-device sync
  • Live click/conversion measurement or confirmed revenue
  • Payment, billing, refund, or payout execution
  • Support cases automatically sent to an operator
  • Physical iPhone completion evidence

Usage limits

No click limit is enforced because the workspace does not collect server-side clicks. Browser storage and device policies may still impose limits.

Live revenue and payment

The current public Free release does not confirm live revenue or execute payment, billing, refunds, or payouts. Those activate only after separate agreement and verification.

Help and next step
