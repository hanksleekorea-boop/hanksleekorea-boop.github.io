About ShoppingScanner

ShoppingScanner is a free discovery and decision tool for exploring observed Korean marketplace product snapshots. It separates confirmed source facts from unknown stock, shipping and option details.

Evidence before claims

Product cards show the merchant source, observed price, observation time and source-ranking context. The service does not claim real-time lowest prices.

Private by default

Core favourites and comparisons stay on the device. Optional account storage requires a separate user action. Advertising and analytics are off by default.

Independent decision support

Affiliate compensation must not determine ranking. The external merchant remains the purchase and fulfilment party.

The legal operator identity and official support route are pending external completion, so this remains a public beta rather than a fully certified commercial launch.
