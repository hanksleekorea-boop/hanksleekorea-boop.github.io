Advertising and affiliate disclosure

The AdSense publisher identity is connected, but third-party ad requests are currently off. They remain off until Google account and site approval, real ad unit IDs, a certified consent provider, operator facts and legal/tax review are verified.

Placement

Approved responsive display, in-feed, in-article or multiplex ads will be labelled “Advertisement” or “Sponsored” and separated from search, filters, comparisons, merchant buttons, accounts, deletion and policy pages. No more than two ad placements are allowed per page. Popunders, forced redirects, anchor or vignette overlays, ad intents, notification mimics and purchase-button ads are prohibited.

Affiliate links

When an approved compensated link is enabled, ShoppingScanner may receive a commission after a qualifying purchase. The possibility will be disclosed near the link and must not change product ranking.

Merchant boundary

The external merchant remains responsible for price, stock, checkout, payment, shipping, cancellation, refund and customer support.

Your choice

Optional advertising personalisation and analytics are denied by default. Core discovery remains available after rejection.
