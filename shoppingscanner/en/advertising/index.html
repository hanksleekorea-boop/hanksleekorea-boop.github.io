Advertising and affiliate disclosure

Third-party display ads and affiliate tracking are currently off. They remain off until real publisher IDs, network approvals, a certified consent provider, operator facts and legal/tax review are complete.

Placement

Approved ads will be labelled “Advertisement” or “Sponsored” and separated from search, filters, comparisons, merchant buttons, accounts, deletion and policy pages. Popunders, forced redirects, notification mimics and purchase-button ads are prohibited.

Affiliate links

When an approved compensated link is enabled, ShoppingScanner may receive a commission after a qualifying purchase. The possibility will be disclosed near the link and must not change product ranking.

Merchant boundary

The external merchant remains responsible for price, stock, checkout, payment, shipping, cancellation, refund and customer support.

Your choice

Optional advertising personalisation and analytics are denied by default. Core discovery remains available after rejection.
