Cookie and storage choices

Optional use is denied by default. No approved advertising or analytics provider is currently configured.

Necessary storage

Keeps privacy choices, language, accessibility settings and user-requested local product features. It is not used for advertising personalisation.

Optional advertising storage

May be used by an approved provider for ad selection, frequency control and measurement after activation. Refusing it does not block search or comparison.

Optional analytics storage

May support aggregate service measurement only after an analytics provider and retention policy are formally configured.

Global Privacy Control

A Global Privacy Control signal is honoured as an opt-out from optional advertising and analytics use.
